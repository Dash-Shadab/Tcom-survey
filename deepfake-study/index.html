Research Objective

Evaluation of Deepfake Image Detection Training

This empirical research study investigates whether a short, structured educational session improves an individual's ability to distinguish authentic photographs from AI-generated synthetic images.

Strict Privacy & Anonymity Protocol

To protect participant privacy, we do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or IP addresses. Your responses will be identified exclusively by an anonymous, automatically generated code (PID-XXXXX). Participation takes approximately 5–7 minutes.

Used strictly for aggregate demographic comparisons.

Study Structure

  1. Baseline Assessment: You will evaluate 5 images (one per screen) as either REAL or AI-GENERATED.
  2. Detection Training: A concise visual awareness guide detailing common synthetic anomalies and optical cues.
  3. Follow-up Assessment: You will evaluate 5 fresh images to assess detection accuracy.
